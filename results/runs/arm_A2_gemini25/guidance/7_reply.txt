Your most successful changes, leading to design ea244a758864, have come from modifying `victim.bypass_when` with new condition words like `pred_exceeds_blended_etr`. This strategy of comparing an incoming line's predicted value against an average of resident lines has yielded gains. However, this approach is not precise enough, as shown by the large number of `wrong_bypass` mistakes on high-headroom traces like `605.mcf_s` and `459.GemsFDTD-1491B`, and `kept_dead` mistakes on `619.lbm_s`. These errors occur because an average-based decision can bypass a line that is better than the chosen victim or keep a dead line instead of a more valuable one. To address this, you need to move beyond averages and focus on the direct interaction between the incoming line and the victim. The vocabulary lacks a simple condition to compare an incoming line's predicted ETR directly against the victim's ETR. A new word expressing this comparison could reduce both `wrong_bypass` and `kept_dead` mistakes. You must stop modifying `procedures.access` and `signatures`, which have only hurt performance.
