You are wasting the majority of your proposals on basic errors; you must stop trying to redefine existing words like `train_sampler_match` and you must use valid, lowercase names instead of constructs like `mockingjay_confidence_full_clock`. Your most effective changes have been defining new words, such as `deconfident_exceeds_victim`, that combine reuse distance and confidence, and using them to alter `victim.bypass_when`. However, changes to `signatures` and `on_hit` have consistently reduced the score and should be avoided. This bypass strategy remains inaccurate, causing a high number of `wrong_bypass` mistakes on traces with significant headroom like `605.mcf_s` and `619.lbm_s`. On `627.cam4_s`, the trace with the most room for improvement, the prevalence of `kept_dead` mistakes proves your ranking of resident lines is also flawed. Both mistake types stem from poor reuse prediction, as your main training step `train_sampler_match` only updates the `rdp_table` and does not learn from outcomes to train confidence. To address this, you need to penalize confidence for mispredictions. The vocabulary currently lacks a way to do this upon eviction. Propose a new `step` word that runs when a line is evicted to decrement the confidence for its signature if the line was predicted to be valuable but was evicted without being reused.
